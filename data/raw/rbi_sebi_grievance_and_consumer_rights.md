# RBI, SEBI & IRDAI - Grievance Redressal and Consumer Protection Master Guide

## What is the RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (RB-IOS)?
The Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (RB-IOS) provides a single, cost-free grievance redressal mechanism for customers of commercial banks, regional rural banks, scheduled primary cooperative banks, non-banking financial companies (NBFCs), and payment system participants. If a bank fails to respond to a customer complaint within 30 days or rejects it unsatisfactorily, the customer can escalate the dispute to the RBI Ombudsman online at cms.rbi.org.in or by toll-free phone 14448. The Ombudsman can award compensation up to ₹20,00,000 for loss suffered and up to ₹1,00,000 for mental agony and harassment.

## What is the Customer Zero-Liability Rule for Unauthorized Electronic Banking Fraud?
Under RBI Master Directions on Customer Protection:
1. **Zero Liability**: If an unauthorized electronic transaction occurs due to contributory fraud, negligence, or deficiency on the part of the bank, or third-party breach where the customer reports the incident within 3 working days of receiving bank SMS/email alert.
2. **Limited Liability**: If the customer reports within 4 to 7 working days, maximum liability is limited between ₹5,000 and ₹25,000 depending on account type.
3. **Beyond 7 days**: Liability is determined according to the bank's approved board policy.
Upon being notified, the bank must credit the shadow reversal into the customer's account within 10 working days.

## What is SEBI SCORES (SEBI Complaints Redress System)?
SEBI Complaints Redress System (SCORES) is a centralized web-based complaint redressal platform operated by SEBI that enables retail stock and mutual fund investors to lodge grievances against listed companies, stockbrokers, depository participants, asset management companies, and registrars (RTAs). Entities are mandated to resolve grievances within 21 calendar days, failing which automatic escalation to SEBI officials occurs.

## What is IRDAI Bima Bharosa for Insurance Grievances?
Bima Bharosa (formerly Integrated Grievance Management System / IGMS) is a regulatory platform established by IRDAI that provides a centralized repository of all insurance policyholder grievances across life, health, and general insurance companies in India. If an insurer does not resolve a claim rejection, mis-selling, or delayed settlement dispute within 15 days, policyholders can escalate the grievance through Bima Bharosa or approach the Insurance Ombudsman.
