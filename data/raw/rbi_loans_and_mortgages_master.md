# Reserve Bank of India - Master Direction on Loans, Mortgages, and Borrower Guidelines

## What is a Home Loan and How Does It Work?
A Home Loan (housing loan) is a secured credit facility provided by banks and Housing Finance Companies (HFCs) to purchase, construct, or renovate residential properties. The property being financed serves as primary collateral mortgage to the lender until the entire loan principal and accrued interest are fully repaid. Home loan tenures can extend up to 30 years with Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratios ranging between 75% and 90% depending on the loan amount.

## Fixed Interest Rate vs Floating Interest Rate Loans
A Fixed Rate Loan maintains a constant interest rate throughout the loan tenure, providing predictable EMI payments regardless of macroeconomic rate changes. A Floating Rate (Variable Rate) Loan is pegged to an external regulatory benchmark (such as the RBI Repo Rate or Treasury Bill yield). When the RBI adjusts the benchmark repo rate, the borrowing rate and monthly EMI or loan tenure automatically adjust.

## What is Loan Foreclosure and Prepayment Penalty Rule?
Prepayment refers to paying a portion of the loan principal before the scheduled due date, while Foreclosure refers to settling the full outstanding balance and closing the loan account early. According to RBI guidelines, banks, NBFCs, and housing finance companies are strictly prohibited from charging any prepayment penalties or foreclosure fees on floating-rate term loans sanctioned to individual borrowers for non-business purposes.

## What is an Education Loan and Moratorium Period?
An Education Loan is a targeted loan facility to finance tuition fees, living expenses, and study materials for higher education in India or abroad. A key feature is the 'moratorium period' (repayment holiday), which covers the complete course duration plus 6 months to 1 year after course completion or upon getting employment, during which the student is not obligated to pay EMIs.

## What is the Reality of "Zero Cost EMI" or "No Cost EMI"?
Under RBI circulars, there is no such thing as truly "free" interest credit. In zero-cost EMI retail schemes, the interest charge is either built into the upfront product price (by denying the cash discount that upfront buyers receive) or passed on as an upfront merchant discount equivalent to the interest that the bank charges over the loan tenure. Furthermore, GST at 18% is applicable on the interest component billed by the lender.

## How Do Credit Scores (CIBIL) Impact Loan Approval and Interest Rates?
Credit Information Companies (CICs) such as TransUnion CIBIL, Experian, and Equifax score borrowers on a scale of 300 to 900 based on repayment track record (35%), credit utilization ratio (30%), credit history length (15%), credit mix (10%), and new credit inquiries (10%). A CIBIL score of 750 or higher qualifies borrowers for preferential low interest rates, higher loan amounts, and waived processing charges.
