# SEBI & AMFI - Comprehensive Investor Guide on Mutual Funds and Portfolio Types

## What are Equity Mutual Funds, Debt Mutual Funds, and Hybrid Mutual Funds?
Equity Mutual Funds invest at least 65% of their total assets in stocks and equity-related instruments of publicly traded companies, suitable for long-term wealth creation with higher market volatility. Debt Mutual Funds invest in fixed-income securities such as Treasury Bills, Government Securities (G-Secs), Certificates of Deposit (CDs), and Corporate Bonds, aiming for capital preservation and stable returns. Hybrid Mutual Funds invest in a balanced mix of equity and debt assets to optimize risk and return.

## What is an Index Fund vs an Actively Managed Mutual Fund?
An Actively Managed Mutual Fund relies on a professional fund manager and research team who make strategic buy/sell decisions to outperform a benchmark index (e.g. NIFTY 50 or S&P BSE SENSEX). An Index Fund (Passive Fund) is an unmanaged fund that replicates the exact composition and weightings of a target market index, resulting in lower management fees, zero manager bias, and minimal tracking error.

## What is Total Expense Ratio (TER) and Direct vs Regular Mutual Fund Plans?
The Total Expense Ratio (TER) is the annual percentage of a mutual fund scheme's daily net assets charged by the Asset Management Company (AMC) to cover fund management fees, administrative costs, and audit charges. A Direct Plan is purchased directly from the AMC without an intermediary distributor, resulting in lower TER (often 0.5% to 1.5% lower) and a higher compound NAV over time. A Regular Plan includes distributor brokerage commissions paid out of the fund's corpus, leading to lower net returns.

## What is Net Asset Value (NAV) and Exit Load?
Net Asset Value (NAV) is the market value per unit of a mutual fund scheme, calculated daily after deducting liabilities and expenses from total market assets: NAV = (Total Assets - Liabilities) / Outstanding Units. An Exit Load is a fee or penalty charged by the AMC when an investor redeems or switches units before a specified lock-in duration (e.g., 1% exit load if redeemed within 1 year) to discourage short-term speculation.

## What is an ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme)?
An Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS) is a tax-saving diversified equity mutual fund that qualifies for tax deduction up to ₹1,50,000 under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act (Old Tax Regime). ELSS has the shortest mandatory lock-in period among all Section 80C tax-saving instruments (only 3 years, compared to 5 years for Tax-Saving FDs and 15 years for PPF), offering potential equity returns combined with tax relief.
