# National Payments Corporation of India & RBI - UPI and Digital Payments

## What is UPI and How Does It Work?
Unified Payments Interface (UPI) is an instant real-time payment system developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) and regulated by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). It powers multiple bank accounts into a single mobile application, merging several banking features, seamless fund routing, and merchant payments into one two-factor authenticated platform. UPI facilitates peer-to-peer (P2P) and peer-to-merchant (P2M) transactions 24x7x365.

## What is a UPI PIN and Virtual Payment Address (VPA)?
A Virtual Payment Address (VPA), also known as a UPI ID, is a unique financial identifier (e.g. username@bank) that allows users to send and receive money without disclosing their confidential bank account number or IFSC code. A UPI PIN is a 4-digit or 6-digit personal security passcode created by the account holder using their debit card details, which must be entered to authorize any debit transaction. You NEVER need to enter a UPI PIN to receive money.

## What are UPI Transaction Limits and Charges?
Under NPCI guidelines, the standard transaction limit for P2P UPI transfers is ₹1,00,000 (Rupees One Lakh) per transaction and per day, though individual banks may set lower internal thresholds. For specific categories such as capital markets, collections, insurance, and educational institutions, the transaction limit is extended up to ₹2,00,000 to ₹5,00,000. Peer-to-peer UPI transactions are free of charge for retail consumers.

## What is UPI Lite and UPI 123Pay?
UPI Lite is an on-device wallet feature enabling small-value transactions up to ₹500 without requiring a UPI PIN, operating with a maximum wallet balance of ₹2,000 to reduce bank server load and transaction failures. UPI 123Pay is an offline payment solution for feature phone users without internet access, allowing transactions through IVR (Interactive Voice Response) numbers, missed call payments, OEM-embedded apps, and sound-based proximity technologies.

## What to Do in Case of UPI Transaction Failures or Fraud?
If an account is debited but the beneficiary does not receive the funds, the payment typically auto-reverses via the Auto-Reversal Mechanism within T+1 working days. Under RBI guidelines on customer protection, if money is debited due to unauthorized third-party transactions and the customer notifies the bank within 3 working days, the customer has zero liability and is entitled to a full refund.
