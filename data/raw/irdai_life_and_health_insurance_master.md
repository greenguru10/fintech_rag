# Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) - Life and Health Insurance Master Guide

## Term Life Insurance vs Whole Life Insurance and ULIPs
Term Insurance is a pure risk protection policy that provides a high sum assured death benefit to beneficiaries if the policyholder passes away during the policy term, with zero maturity payout if the insured survives. Whole Life Insurance covers the insured up to age 99 or 100 with guaranteed payouts. Unit Linked Insurance Plans (ULIPs) combine insurance cover with equity/debt market investments, but carry higher upfront allocation charges and fund management fees compared to standalone term plans and mutual funds.

## What is Room Rent Capping, Co-Payment, and Deductibles in Health Insurance?
Room Rent Capping is a policy clause that restricts the maximum room category eligible for coverage (e.g. 1% of Sum Insured per day). If a patient chooses a room above the capping limit, all associated medical bills (doctor visits, nursing fees, surgical charges) are subject to proportionate deductions. Co-Payment is a clause where the policyholder agrees to pay a fixed percentage (e.g. 10% or 20%) of the total admissible claim out of pocket. A Deductible is a fixed sum that must be paid before insurance coverage begins.

## What are Pre-Existing Diseases (PED) and Waiting Periods?
A Pre-Existing Disease (PED) is any medical condition, ailment, or injury diagnosed or treated prior to buying a health insurance policy (e.g. diabetes, hypertension, asthma). Under IRDAI regulations, health insurers enforce a mandatory Waiting Period (typically 2 to 3 years) during which claims related to declared pre-existing diseases are not covered. After completion of the waiting period without policy lapse, PED claims are fully admissible.

## What is a No Claim Bonus (NCB) and Restoration Benefit?
A No Claim Bonus (NCB) is a reward given to policyholders for every claim-free year, typically increasing the cumulative Sum Insured by 10% to 50% per claim-free year without increasing the annual premium, up to a maximum limit of 100% to 200%. A Restoration Benefit (Refill Benefit) automatically reinstates the full Sum Insured if the original policy cover is exhausted due to hospitalization during the policy year.

## What is Claim Settlement Ratio (CSR) and Cashless vs Reimbursement Hospitalization?
The Claim Settlement Ratio (CSR) indicates the percentage of claims successfully settled by an insurer out of total claims received during a financial year (a CSR of 95%+ is considered strong). Under Cashless Hospitalization, the insurer or Third Party Administrator (TPA) settles medical bills directly with network hospitals via pre-authorization approval. Under Reimbursement Claims, the insured pays the hospital directly and submits discharge summaries, bills, and payment receipts for reimbursement within 15–30 days.
